# Engineering (Horde)

Route from wow-professions.com (Forever version). Icy Veins' 1–75 list (search summary only, page blocked) asks for ~60 Rough Stone, 66 Copper Bar, 45 Linen: budget a few spares.

## User check: "≈60 Copper Bars, 56 Rough Stone to skill 75"

**Confirmed, slightly low on bars.** 1–75 needs **56 Rough Stone, 62 Copper Bar, 28 Linen Cloth**, plus a Blacksmith Hammer. Add for camping: Lodestone (1 bar + 1 stone, Mining) and Reagent Bot (1 Handful of Copper Bolts + 1 Copper Tube). Safe target: **~66–70 bars, ~58 stone, ~30 linen**.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1–35 | Rough Blasting Powder × 56 | 56 Rough Stone | Keep all 56 (bombs) |

| 100–105 | Silver Contact × 5 | 5 Silver Bar (alt: 5 Flying Tiger Goggles: 30 Light Leather, 10 Tigerseye) | |
| 105–125 | Bronze Tube × 25 | 50 Bronze Bar, 25 Weak Flux | Keep 10 |
| 125–135 | Standard Scope × 10 | 10 Bronze Tube, 10 Moss Agate (alt: Heavy Blasting Powder) | |

| 135–150 | Whirring Bronze Gizmo × 15 | 30 Bronze Bar, 15 Wool Cloth | Keep |

Craft the last two in parallel so both are done at 150.

**Totals 1–150:** 56 Rough Stone, 62 Copper Bar, 45 Linen, 51 Coarse Stone, 5 Silver Bar, 80 Bronze Bar, 25 Weak Flux (engineering vendor), 10 Moss Agate, 40 Heavy Stone, 15 Wool.

| 150–160 | Bronze Framework × 15 | 30 Bronze Bar, 15 Medium Leather, 15 Wool |

| 190–195 | Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor (keep) + Iron Grenade × 10 | 4 Steel Bar; 10 Iron Bar, 10 Heavy Powder, 10 Silk |
| 195–200 | Mithril Tube × 6 | 18 Mithril Bar (keep for Gnomish) |
| 200–250 | Unstable Trigger, Mithril Casing, Hi-Explosive Bomb | Mithril, Mageweave, Solid Powder |
| 250–300 | Dense Blasting Powder, Thorium Widget, Thorium Tube | 88 Dense Stone, 186 Thorium Bar, 20 Runecloth |

Thorium Tube schematic is bind-on-pickup (Xizzer Fizzbolt, Winterspring, or Kor'gar, Riverglades). Specialization (Gnomish / Goblin) at Engineering 200; switching means relearning from scratch.
