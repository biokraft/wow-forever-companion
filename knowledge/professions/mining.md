# Mining (Horde)

## Forever changes

- Mining (gathering) can be learned at level 1. Journeyman needs level 10, Expert 20, Artisan 35 (all professions).
- Poor Copper Ore veins in starter zones: level 1 characters can mine copper and Rough Stone.
- Dynamic node respawn: more players nearby → faster respawn. Some caves respawn fast enough to stay inside.
- Find Minerals works alongside other tracking. It switches off on death; re-enable it.
- New ores and bars: Pyrite, Bauxite, Pitchblende, Azerothium Bar (smelt from 275), Heavy Thorium Bar. Skill ranges for the new ores: unverified.
- A vein gives a skill-up only on its first successful mine.
- Camp object **Lodestone** (Mining 20): 1 Rough Stone + 1 Copper Bar. Attack Power buff like Blessing of Might (does not stack with it). Recipe from the "Camping 101: Mining" starter quest or any mining trainer. Tier 2 Rock Garden spawns common ore; tier 3 Molten Foundry is needed for high-end smelting.
- Rockslicer (Rhahk'Zor, Deadmines): 30-use pick that mines copper to iron without the profession.
- Legacy perk Bountiful Harvest: more Scarce materials from gathering.

## Leveling by buying ore (if gold allows)

1–65: smelt ~140 Copper Ore (keep 70 bars), 65–75: 70 Tin Bars, 75–105: 70 Bronze Bars, 105–120: 35–45 Silver Bars. Bars often sell near ore prices. Mining nodes beats smelting from 120 up.

## Advice for this user (Mining + Engineering)

- Keep all Copper Ore and Rough Stone early. Engineering 1–75 needs ~62 Copper Bars and 56 Rough Stone (see `engineering.md`), the Lodestone needs 1 of each, the Reagent Bot needs a Copper Tube.
- Engineering also needs 51 Coarse Stone (75–90) and 40 Heavy Stone (135–150). In Classic these come from tin and iron veins respectively (model memory, `scope: classic`, unverified for Forever).
