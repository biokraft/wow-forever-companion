# Cooking (Horde)

## Why it matters in Forever

- **Well Fed = +5% kill XP** (not quest XP), about 15 min, lost on death, one Well Fed at a time. "Most" or "all" cooked food qualifies (sources differ): check the tooltip. 23 Nov hotfix fixed clam dishes' Well Fed and moved them to "Stamina Food".
- **Campfires** are Cooking items: learn the kit, place the fire from bags (stack kits in advance).

| Campfire | Cooking skill | Materials | Camp object slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Campfire | 1 | Flint and Tinder + Simple Wood | 3 |
| Journeyman Campfire | **90** (was 140 until 10 Nov) | Flint and Tinder + Star Wood | 5 |
| Expert Campfire | 220 (WT) / 200 (games.gg): conflict | Flint and Tinder + Thick Logs | 10 |

Basic Campfire burns ~10 min, 5 min cooldown (WT). Keep the Flint and Tinder (wow-professions says to bank it; whether it is consumed: unverified). It is also a Cozy Sleeping Bag chain reward. Whether the campfire alone gives rested XP: unclear (see `forever-rules.md`).

Campfire kit vendors (Horde): Xen'to (Orgrimmar), Jark (Razor Hill barracks), Naal Mistrunner (Thunder Bluff), Ronald Burch (Undercity), Moorat Longstride (Bloodhoof).

## Advice

- Always carry cooked food for the Well Fed XP buff; re-eat after deaths.
- Mixing routes is fine: cook whatever meat drops on the route (boar → bear → dig rat), buy fish on the AH to fill gaps.
- Get Cooking to 90 early for the Journeyman Campfire (5 slots, room for group camp buffs).
