# Warrior leveling (Horde, Troll, Arms two-hander)

Forever changed the Warrior a lot (rage, talents, new abilities). Classic leveling guides are only partly valid. Lines tagged `scope: classic` come from Classic sources and were not confirmed for Forever.

## Forever changes that affect leveling

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rage from hits | Based on weapon **speed**, not damage. Crits give 75% more rage (deep dive); a beta note says 100% (unverified for live). |
| Rage from damage taken | Ignores armor and absorbs |
| Rage overall | Warcraft Tavern calls it very low compared to Classic: spend early on Rend/Sunder, Charge for an opener |
| Victory Rush (lvl 20) | No rage cost, 30 s cooldown, heals 10% max HP; usable 20 s after killing a non-trivial enemy |
| Tactical Mastery (lvl 14) | Now a trainer ability: keep 10 rage on stance swap. Talent Improved Tactical Mastery adds more. |
| Rend, Thunder Clap, Sunder Armor | More attack power scaling (Rend is a real damage tool now) |
| Battle Shout | Smaller AP bonus; Improved Battle Shout and Improved Demoralizing Shout are baseline |
| Slam | 15 s cooldown (was 18) since 3 Nov |
| Mortal Strike | Talent only (23 Nov hotfix stopped untalented use from the spellbook) |
| Weapon skills | Some weapon-skill bonuses replaced by Expertise (all classes) |
| Retaliation / Shield Wall / Recklessness | No longer share a cooldown; Retaliation and Shield Wall 15 min |
| Berserker Rage | Removes Sap (13 Nov) |
| Sweeping Strikes | No longer loses charges with no second target (10 Nov) |
| Class difficulty | Warrior was among the slowest classes to 60 in beta; struggles vs higher-level mobs and packs (Method) |

## Stat priority (leveling)

1. Weapon: slow two-hander with high top-end damage (instant attacks like Mortal Strike and Whirlwind scale with weapon damage), then DPS.
2. Strength (2 melee AP per point; 14 AP = 1 DPS, so 1 weapon DPS ≈ 7 Str for white hits).
3. Stamina.
4. Agility (crit and armor; no melee AP for warriors).
5. Armor.

Crit is worth more than in Classic: extra rage on crits, Deep Wounds, and +5% crit from Weaponmaster with axes or polearms. Sources: Warcraft Tavern (order), Timesaver (crit), wiki Attack power (formula, `scope: classic`).

## Tanking basics (dungeons)

- Defensive Stance threat bonus is **80%** (was 30%) since 3 Nov. Defiance (Protection talent) now 7/13/20% and needs a shield.
- Thunder Clap works in Defensive Stance (6 s cd): main pack-threat tool early. Sunder Armor scales with AP.
- Tactical Mastery keeps 10 rage on swaps, so Charge in Battle Stance → swap to Defensive is cheap. Vanguard (Protection) allows Charge in Defensive Stance.
- Rage from taking damage ignores armor and shields, so tanking gives steady rage.
- Pull order: mark kill target, Sunder it, Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout for packs, Taunt / Mocking Blow (16) for loose mobs, Challenging Shout (26) and Shield Wall (28) as emergency buttons. Classic practice, `scope: classic`.
- An Arms two-hander can tank lower dungeons in Defensive Stance; with a shield, Shield Block (16) and Shield Bash (12) become available. Keep a cheap one-hand + shield in bags.
- Dungeon XP rule: if a party member is more than 12 levels above the mobs, nobody gets kill XP (see `forever-rules.md`).
