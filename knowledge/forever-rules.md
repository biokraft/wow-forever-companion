# WoW: Forever rules that matter for leveling

Forever launched 4 Nov 2026 (EU/US). Hotfixes land several times a week; anything dated below can change again. For a recent rule, do a fresh web check of the Blizzard hotfix thread (first source).

## Status of rules the user reported

| User report | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Well Fed food ≈ +5% kill XP | **Confirmed** | +5% XP from kills, not quest XP. About 15 min, lost on death. Only one Well Fed buff at a time. "Most" / "all" cooked food (sources differ): check the tooltip. Clam dishes fixed 23 Nov. |
| Campfires give rested XP | **Partly / unclear** | The Basic Campfire itself is not listed as a rested area in Warcraft Tavern's experience and camping guides. Only one beta-era article says it "grants rested status" (PC Tech Mag). Confirmed rested sources in the field: **Cozy Sleeping Bag** (counts as a rested area while you lie in it) and **Camp Tent** (Leatherworking 20; tops rested XP up to 5% of a level, 1 h "Boosted Rest" lockout; needs a campfire nearby). |
| Cozy Sleeping Bag chain from level 14, starting with "Stepping Stones" | **Corrected** | Level 14 is right. The Horde chain **starts** with "...and that note you found" at the Burned-Out Remains, south Barrens (46, 74, south of Camp Taurajo). "Stepping Stones" is the **second** quest, picked up in **Westfall** (Nailed Plank, 37, 50). |
| Dungeon kill XP +50% since 9 Nov 2026 | **Confirmed** | US post: deployed 8 Nov 11:05 PST; listed under 9 Nov in the EU hotfix thread. Applies to all dungeon enemy kills. |
| No dungeon kill XP if any group member out-levels the mob (since 13 Nov) | **Outdated** | 13 Nov hotfix: zero XP for the whole party if an enemy is trivial (grey) to any member. Revised in the 14/15 Nov hotfix: XP is nullified only if a party member is **more than 12 levels above** the enemy. |
| Legacy system opens at first level 25, "Well Rested" pick | **Confirmed** | First Legacy Point unlocks the UI (hotkey Y): first character to 25 **or** 150 in a non-gathering crafting profession (Engineering counts). Well Rested: 5 ranks, each +4% rested accumulation and +4% rested cap. |
| Dual spec at 40 | **Confirmed (level), cost unverified** | Listed at the level-40 trainer row ("Dual Talent Specialization") in Warcraft Tavern's Forever trainer table. No Forever price found. |

## XP basics

| Rule | Value | Source note |
|---|---|---|
| XP curve | Unchanged from Classic. 4,084,700 XP for 1→60. Level 10→11: 7,600; 30→31: 47,400; 59→60: 209,800. | WT experience guide, Rankedboost |
| Rested accumulation | 5% of the current level's XP per 8 h in a rested area | WT experience guide |
| Rested cap | 150% of a level (30 bubbles); ~10 days logged out | WT experience guide |
| Rested effect | Doubles kill XP; no effect on quest or exploration XP | WT experience guide |
| Group XP (5-man) | Kill XP ÷ 5 × 1.4 (100 solo XP → 28 each); quest XP unaffected | WT experience guide |
| Grey mobs | No XP | WT experience guide |
| Grey quests | 5+ levels below: XP cut 10–20% per extra level | WT experience guide |
| Mob level | Higher-level mobs give more XP than lower ones | WT experience guide |
| Quest gold | Most quests level 48+ also reward gold (10 Nov) | Blizzard hotfix thread |
| Content | 1,000+ new quests; Blizzard says new zones add questing where Classic slowed down | PC Tech Mag |

## Cozy Sleeping Bag chain (Horde), level 14+

No combat needed, but it crosses both continents. Order per Warcraft Tavern:

1. **"...and that note you found"**: Burned-Out Remains, south Barrens (46, 74).
2. Turn in at Nailed Plank, Westfall (37, 50) → rewards Swiftness Potion, Lesser Troll's Blood Potion → starts **"Stepping Stones"**.
3. Stonetalon Mountains: Pocket Litter, camp down a hill path near Sun Rock Retreat → Flint and Tinder, 3 Simple Wood, Sturdy Lunchbox, Mining Pick or Farmer's Shovel → starts **"Scramble"** → Mound of Dirt just east → ammo + gun/bow/thrown choice → starts **"Wet Job"**.
4. Loch Modan: Carved Figurine at Stonewrought Dam (49, 12), ledge on the dam → starts **"Eagle's Fist"**. Route for Horde: zeppelin to Undercity → Silverpine → Hillsbrad → Arathi → Wetlands → Loch Modan (level-30 guards on the way).
5. Hillsbrad Foothills: climb onto Thoradin's Wall via the cart, Messenger Bag, then Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel → **"This Must Be the Place"** → **Cozy Sleeping Bag**.

Advisor note: each quest starts the next, so the order is fixed. For a Barrens-route Horde player, the Westfall (step 2) and Loch Modan (step 4) legs are long detours; the Hillsbrad finish fits the 20s route.

## Dungeon XP timeline

| Date (2026) | Change |
|---|---|
| 1 Oct (beta) | Dungeon quest bonus XP halved |
| 8 Oct (beta) | Dungeon mob XP +~20% |
| 8 Nov US / 9 Nov EU | All dungeon kill XP **+50%** |
| 13 Nov | Enemies trivial to any party member gave 0 XP to the whole party |
| 14–15 Nov | Revised: 0 XP only when a party member is **>12 levels above** the enemy. Did not apply to instances already in progress. |
| 20 Nov | Instance lockout: 5 dungeon entries per hour (was 10) |

Advisor guidance from sources: run each dungeon once while it is in range; farming one dungeon for ten levels is not expected to work (PC Tech Mag). Nine new dungeons, roughly 13–60 (e.g. Hall of Thanes 13–18, Alliance side).

## Legacy system (account-wide)

- Points: 1 per class at 25 / 45 / 60; 1 per crafting profession (Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring) at 150 / 225 / 300; plus PvP, exploration, dungeon and raid challenges.
- Cooking, First Aid, Mining are **not** in the profession-points list (unverified whether other challenges cover them).
- Spendable per character at launch: **16 points**.
- Other leveling perks: Bartering (−5% vendor prices, 2 ranks), Frequent Flier (−50% flight cost), Reagent Economy (no vendor reagents for class spells), Working Overtime (+4% skill-up chance per rank).

## Professions (leveling-relevant)

- Training tiers: Apprentice (gathering lvl 1, crafting/secondary lvl 5), Journeyman lvl 10, Expert lvl 20, Artisan lvl 35. Cap 300.
- Camping: Cooking makes campfires (kit items placed from bags). Basic Campfire holds 3 camp objects; Journeyman Campfire (Cooking 90 since 10 Nov, was 140) holds 5; Expert holds 10. One object per character, 1 h cooldown; rest 1 min at the camp for buffs.
- Useful camp buffs for a warrior: Lodestone (Mining 20, Attack Power like Blessing of Might), First Aid Kit (First Aid 20, Stamina like Fortitude), Sharpening Wheel (Blacksmithing, Strength), Camp Chair (Skinning, 2% crit), Camp Tent (rested XP).
- Healing potions moved from Alchemy to First Aid.

## Other rules worth knowing

- Realmless: choose Normal, PvP or RP ruleset; groups can't span rulesets.
- Warrior and Priest were the slowest classes to 60 in beta; Hunter fastest (PC Tech Mag).
- Find Minerals can be tracked together with other tracking.
- Learning a spell no longer auto-places it on the action bar (3 Nov).
